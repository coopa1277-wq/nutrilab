The Founder

"I spent years studying pharmacology and watching people spend hundreds of dollars on supplements that were never going to work — not because the ingredients were wrong, but because of how they take them."

After completing an MSc in Pharmacology, our founder became increasingly frustrated with the gap between what the clinical literature showed and what was actually available on supplement shelves. Personally, watching people she cared about spend real money on products dosed at fractions of what the research actually supported, with no way of knowing the difference.

The science was clear. The products on the shelf were not.

NutriLab was built to close that gap. Every ingredient in our 172-ingredient library was selected and ranked based on the strength of its clinical evidence. Every dose is calibrated to the range used in peer-reviewed trials. And every formula is reviewed by a licensed pharmacist before it reaches you.

Our core principles

Evidence first, always

Every ingredient is selected based on peer-reviewed RCTs and meta-analyses. No trends, no fads, just peer-reviewed science.

Therapeutic doses, not label doses

There is a legal minimum needed to print a claim on a label, and there is the dose actually used in clinical trials. We use the latter — every time.

Personalized, not generic

Your health concerns, your symptom severity, your medication list. Your formula is built around you specifically.

Pharmacist reviewed, every time

Before your formula is prepared, a licensed pharmacist reviews your ingredient selection and screens for interactions with your medications.

Health Canada compliant

Every ingredient in our library is a confirmed Health Canada Schedule 1 Natural Health Product.

Radical transparency

No proprietary blends. No hidden doses. Every ingredient comes with its dose, mechanism, and the clinical evidence behind it.

Zero fillers. Zero unknowns.

NutriLab formulas are compounded fresh with no fillers, no binders, no artificial coatings — and a certificate of analysis for every ingredient.

Allergy-safe by design

No hidden excipients and no cross-contamination from irrelevant ingredients — safe for people with allergies.

What is Natural Health Product compounding?

Compounding is the practice of creating a personalized Natural Health Product (NHP) by a licensed pharmacist — tailored to the specific needs of an individual rather than mass-produced.

Health Canada regulated

NutriLab formulas are compounded in accordance with Health Canada's NHP Compounding Policy at CompoundIt Pharmacy — a licensed NHP compounding facility in Aurora, Ontario.

Why it matters

Compounding allows your dose to be calibrated precisely to your symptom severity, allergen profile, and medication interactions — something no mass-produced product can offer.
