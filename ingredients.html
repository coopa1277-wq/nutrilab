Sleep
These ingredients support your body's natural sleep rhythms — helping you fall asleep more easily, stay asleep, and wake up feeling restored.
7 ingredients
Sleep Onset / Circadian Rhythm
Melatonin
Melatonin
Your body's natural sleep signal
Melatonin is a hormone your brain already produces at night to signal bedtime. A small supplement dose (0.5 mg) gently reinforces that signal — especially helpful if you struggle to fall asleep, work irregular hours, or travel across time zones. Non-addictive and works with your body's own rhythm.
Consult your doctor if you take prescription sleep medication or sedatives (e.g. Ativan, clonazepam, Zopiclone).
0.5–1 mg PM
L-Theanine
L-Theanine
Calms the mind without causing drowsiness
Found naturally in green tea, L-Theanine creates a calm, focused state without sedation. Think of it as a gentle off-switch for an overactive mind — equally useful at bedtime to wind down or during the day to reduce anxious mental chatter.
200 mg PM
L-Tryptophan
L-Tryptophan
The amino acid your body converts into sleep hormones
Tryptophan is why a warm glass of milk can make you sleepy — your body converts it into serotonin and then melatonin, your natural sleep hormone. Supplementing at bedtime supports the entire upstream sleep production chain.
Avoid if you take antidepressants (SSRIs, MAOIs, SNRIs such as Prozac, Zoloft, Effexor) due to risk of serotonin syndrome.
500–1,000 mg PM
Sleep Maintenance / Cortisol Suppression
Ashwagandha
Ashwagandha 1.5%
Ancient adaptogen for stress and cortisol balance
Ashwagandha helps your body adapt to stress rather than be overwhelmed by it. Clinical trials show it significantly lowers cortisol (the stress hormone) and reduces anxiety. Unlike stimulants, it recalibrates your stress system over several weeks of use.
Consult your doctor if you take thyroid medication (e.g. Synthroid, levothyroxine) or antidepressants.
300 mg PM
GABA
GABA
Your brain's primary calming neurotransmitter
GABA is the main 'brake' signal in the brain — it quiets overexcited nerve activity. When anxiety strikes, the brain often has too little GABA to restore balance. Supplementing helps settle an overactive nervous system for better sleep and calm.
250–500 mg PM
Lemon
Lemon Balm 5:1
A gentle herb for both anxiety and sleeplessness
Lemon balm has been used for centuries to ease restlessness and improve sleep. It works by gently raising GABA levels in the brain — similar in principle to anti-anxiety medications but naturally and without dependence risk.
300–600 mg PM
Hops
Hops 2%
The sleep herb best known from beer — without the alcohol
Hop flowers contain natural compounds that calm the nervous system and shorten the time to fall asleep. Works especially well combined with lemon balm, where the two together are more effective than either alone.
300–500 mg PM

SAMe
Replenishes the neurotransmitters that burnout depletes
